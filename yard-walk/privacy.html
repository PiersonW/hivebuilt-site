Privacy Policy

Yard Walk: Beehive Tracker · updated November 14, 2026

Yard Walk is published by Pierson Willhite (Hivebuilt). This page explains what the app does with your information. In short: your records are kept on your phone, backup is optional, and there are no ads and no tracking.

What stays on your phone

Your yards, hives, inspections, notes, mite counts, treatments, to-dos, photos and voice notes are saved on your device first, so the app works with no signal. If you do not turn on backup, they never leave your phone, and deleting the app deletes them.

What is sent, and why

  • Your yard's location, rounded to about a kilometre, is sent to Open-Meteo to log the weather with each inspection. The app asks for your location only while you are inspecting, to remember where the yard is. It does not track you.
  • Backup, only if you turn it on. You make an account with an email address and password. Your records, photos and voice notes are then copied to our server (hosted by Supabase in the United States) so you can restore them on a new phone. Only your account can read them.

Deleting your data

In the app, open More, then Backup, then Delete account. This permanently deletes your backup, including photos and voice notes, and your login. If the same email also signs in to another Hivebuilt app, that login is kept for the other app, but all Yard Walk data is still deleted. Records on your phone are not touched; delete the app to remove them. You can also email us to ask for deletion.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, analytics or tracking, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing.
  • Your email is used only to sign in to backup.
  • The microphone and camera are used only when you tap Voice note or Photo.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

Questions about privacy: hello@hivebuilt.dev. If this policy changes, the date at the top will change.
