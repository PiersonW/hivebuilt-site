Yard Walk

Beehive tracker

Yard Walk is a hive tracker for beekeepers. Walk your bee yard hive by hive, tap what you see, and the app keeps track of what each hive needs next, even where there is no signal.

Help and questions: email hello@hivebuilt.dev. Please say which phone you use and what you were doing when something went wrong.

Common questions

Does it work without signal? Yes. Everything is saved on your phone first. Weather and backup catch up the next time you are online.

How do the notes create to-dos? Some notes act on the hive. Needs mite treatment flags the hive and adds a to-do; Treated for mites clears it and reminds you to recount; Grafted queen cells reminds you to move the cells. Change what any note does, or add your own, under More, Manage notes.

Where does the honey-safe countdown come from? From the number of days you enter from your treatment's label. Yard Walk does not supply withdrawal times; always follow the label.

How do I add nucs or split a hive? On a yard, tap Add hives or nucs, choose how many and which hive they came from. The parent hive's history shows the split.

Is my data backed up? Only if you turn on backup under More, Backup. Then sign in on a new phone to get everything back. You can also export every inspection as a spreadsheet or PDF.

How do I delete my account? More, Backup, Delete account. See the privacy policy for what that removes.
