Rainy Day Pantry

Emergency prep & food storage

Rainy Day Pantry helps your family get ready for an emergency. Tell it who lives with you and how long you want to be ready for, add what you already have stored, and it shows how close you are, what you are short, and what is about to expire.

Help and questions: email hello@hivebuilt.dev. Please say which phone you use and what you were doing when something went wrong.

Common questions

How does it work out what I need? From your household (adults, children, babies, dogs and cats) and your goal: 3 days, 2 weeks, 3 months or a year. Water is figured at a gallon per person per day for up to two weeks. Long-term staples such as grains, beans and oil only appear for goals longer than two weeks.

Can I change a target or skip something? Yes. Open any item to set your own amount or hide it if it does not apply to you.

Why doesn't expired food count? Anything past its use-by date is left out of your totals and listed under To buy as something to replace. Items expiring within two months are listed so you can eat them in everyday meals and buy fresh.

What reminders does it send? A reminder a month before things expire, and a kit check every April and October. Change the time under More, Reminders.

Is my list backed up? No. Everything you enter stays on your phone and is not sent anywhere, so deleting the app deletes your list. Use Share on the To buy tab to send your shopping list to yourself or your family.

Is this official emergency advice? No. The amounts are common planning guidelines. Check with your local emergency management office for advice about your area, and talk to your doctor about medicines.
