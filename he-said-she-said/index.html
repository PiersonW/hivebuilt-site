He Said, She Said

Did they really say that?

One friend answers a question in secret. Another friend passes the answer on, word for word or with a sneaky change. Everyone else has to guess: is that what was really said?

Help and questions: email hello@hivebuilt.dev. Please say which phone you use and what happened.

How to play

  1. One person taps Start a Game and picks the winning score: 10, 15, 20 or 30.
  2. Everyone else enters the 4-letter room code to join. You need at least 4 players. No accounts needed.
  3. Each turn, one player gets a question and types an honest answer that only the messenger sees.
  4. The messenger passes it on as is, picks a fake answer, or writes their own. They tap Done when they've decided.
  5. Everyone else votes: did they really say that, or was it changed?
  6. Guess right and you and the answerer each get a point. Guess wrong and the messenger gets a point.
  7. The first player to reach the winning score wins.

Common questions

Can I try it alone? Yes. In the lobby, tap Fill with computer players. They answer, pass answers on and vote on their own.

What if someone stops playing? Every step has a timer, so nobody can stall. If the answerer doesn't answer in time, the game picks an answer for them, and they don't earn points for it.

What if two players are tied at the winning score? The game keeps going until one of them is ahead.

Are the questions family-friendly? Yes. They're everyday questions like "What would you name a pet goat?" and "What is your go-to karaoke song?"
