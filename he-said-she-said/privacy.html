Privacy Policy

He Said, She Said · updated November 14, 2026

He Said, She Said is published by Pierson Willhite (Hivebuilt). In short: there are no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and game rooms are deleted after two days.

What the app sends, and why

To let several phones play the same game, the app uses a game server hosted by Supabase in the United States. It stores:

  • A random ID for your phone. The app gets one the first time you start or join a game. It is not tied to your name, email, phone number or Apple ID.
  • The name you type, so the other players in your room can see who is answering and voting.
  • The game itself: the room code, the answers players type, the answer the messenger passes on, the votes and the scores.

Only people in the same room can see that room's names, answers, votes and scores. The real answer and the votes stay hidden from other players until each turn's reveal. Rooms are deleted automatically after two days. Computer players get random IDs the same way.

What we do not do

  • No email, phone number, contacts, location, photos or microphone.
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing.

Deleting your data

Leaving a game removes you from it, and every room is deleted two days after it was created. Deleting the app removes the random ID from your phone. To ask us to delete anything sooner, email us.

Children

The questions are family-friendly, but the app does not ask for anyone's age and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Players type only a first name or nickname.

Contact

Questions about privacy: hello@hivebuilt.dev. If this policy changes, the date at the top will change.
