Privacy Policy

Tune-Up · updated November 16, 2026

Tune-Up is published by Pierson Willhite (Hivebuilt). In short: everything you enter stays on your phone. We do not collect any of it, and there are no accounts, ads or tracking.

What stays on your phone

What your home has, your jobs, dates, notes and reminder settings are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your phone. No information is sent to a server to deliver them.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking.
  • Nothing is collected, sold or shared.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not collect personal information from anyone.

Contact

Questions about privacy: hello@hivebuilt.dev. If this policy changes, the date at the top will change.
