Tune-Up

Home & car upkeep, spread out

Tune-Up keeps your house and car in good shape without the overwhelm. Tick what your home has and it builds a year of maintenance jobs, from furnace filters to sprinkler blowouts and oil changes, spread evenly so most weeks you only see one or two.

Help and questions: email hello@hivebuilt.dev. Please say which phone you use and what you were doing when something went wrong.

Common questions

How does it decide when jobs happen? Each job has a usual cycle, such as every 3 months for a furnace filter. Tune-Up spreads all your jobs across your chore day so no week piles up, and keeps seasonal jobs, like a sprinkler blowout, in the right months for your half of the world.

Can I change a job? Yes. Open it to move it or skip it once, change how often it repeats, pause it or remove it. You can also add your own jobs, including weekly ones on a set day.

I added something to my home later. Open More, About your home and tick it, or use Add jobs. Re-spread my year evens everything out again.

I did a job late. Will I end up doing it twice? No. If you tick a job off so late that the next one is less than half a cycle away, that next one is let go.

What reminders does it send? A morning reminder on days a job is due, and if you like, one the evening before. Change them under More.

Is my list backed up? No. Everything stays on your phone and is not sent anywhere, so deleting the app deletes your list.
