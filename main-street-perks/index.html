Main Street Perks

Punch cards for local spots

Main Street Perks puts the punch cards from your favorite local businesses in one app. Customers show one code at the counter; business owners give punches and rewards from their own phone.

Help and questions: email hello@hivebuilt.dev. Please say which phone you use and what you were doing when something went wrong.

For customers

How do I get punches? Find the place in Find and tap the star, then tap Show my code at the counter. Staff scan it and your card updates.

Why does my code change? It changes every few minutes so a screenshot can't be reused by someone else.

I forgot my phone. Add your phone number on the Me tab and staff can look you up by it. Businesses never see your number.

How do I use a reward? When a card is full, show your code and staff can use the reward for you.

For business owners

How do I list my business? Sign in, open For Business, and fill in your business details and your reward: how many punches it takes and what customers get.

How do I give a punch? Tap Scan a customer and point your camera at their code. You can also type the code under their QR or look them up by phone number.

Can people game it? You can set a wait between punches and a daily limit for each customer.

What does it cost? Main Street Perks is free while it gets started. Email us if you'd like help setting up.

Deleting your account

Open Me, then Delete account. This removes your cards, punches and rewards, and any business you own.
