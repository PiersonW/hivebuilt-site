Privacy Policy

Main Street Perks · updated November 16, 2026

Main Street Perks is published by Pierson Willhite (Hivebuilt). This page explains what the app does with your information. In short: we keep only what rewards cards need, and there are no ads and no tracking.

What we keep, and why

  • Your account: an email address and password, used only to sign in, and the first name staff see when they scan your code.
  • Phone number (optional): so staff can look you up if you forgot your phone. Businesses never see it.
  • Your cards: which places you starred, your punches, rewards earned and used, and when.
  • Business owners: the business name, address, map location, phone, hours, description and reward, which customers can see, plus the punches and rewards given.

This is stored on our server, hosted by Supabase in the United States. A business sees the first name, punches and rewards of customers who have a card there, and nothing about your cards at other places.

Location

If you allow it, the app uses your location to sort places by distance. It is sent only to work out distances and is not stored.

Camera

Business owners use the camera to scan customers' codes. Nothing from the camera is saved or sent.

Deleting your data

In the app, open Me, then Delete account. This permanently deletes your profile, cards, punches and rewards, and any business you own with its reward and activity. Your login is also deleted, unless the same email still signs in to another Hivebuilt app. You can also email us to ask for deletion.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, analytics or tracking, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing.
  • Your email is used only to sign in.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

Questions about privacy: hello@hivebuilt.dev. If this policy changes, the date at the top will change.
