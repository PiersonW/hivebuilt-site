Privacy Policy

Porch Stand · updated November 16, 2026

Porch Stand is published by Pierson Willhite (Hivebuilt). This page explains what the app does with your information. In short: shoppers don't make an account, we keep only what the app needs, and there are no ads and no tracking.

Shoppers

  • Your phone gets a random ID (no name or email) so the app can remember the stands you follow and the ones you hid.
  • If you allow notifications, we keep your phone's notification address so stands you follow can let you know when they set out.
  • If you allow location, it is sent only to find stands near you and is not stored.
  • If you report a stand, we keep the report and your random ID so we can look into it.

Stand owners

  • An email address and password, used only to sign in.
  • Your stand's name, description, location on the map, usual days and hours, items and prices, and your Venmo, Cash App or PayPal usernames. These are shown to shoppers.
  • When you set out, restock or put the stand away, so shoppers can see what's out now.

This is stored on our server, hosted by Supabase in the United States. Notifications are delivered through Expo and Apple's or Google's notification service.

Payments

Porch Stand does not process payments. The pay screen adds up a total and opens the owner's payment page in Venmo, Cash App or PayPal, which have their own privacy policies.

Deleting your data

In the app, open My Stand. Owners tap Delete my account, which permanently deletes the stand, its items, its updates and followers, and the login (unless the same email still signs in to another Hivebuilt app). Shoppers tap Delete my data, which removes the stands you follow, the stands you hid and your notification address. You can also email us to ask for deletion.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, analytics or tracking, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

Questions about privacy: hello@hivebuilt.dev. If this policy changes, the date at the top will change.
