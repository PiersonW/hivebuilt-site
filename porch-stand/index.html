Porch Stand

What’s out at farm stands now

Porch Stand shows what the porch stands and farm stands near you have out right now, so you know before you drive over. Stand owners tap to say they're set out and update what's left as it sells.

Help and questions: email hello@hivebuilt.dev. Please say which phone you use and what you were doing when something went wrong.

For shoppers

Do I need an account? No. Open the app and browse. Your phone gets a random ID so it can remember the stands you follow.

How do I know when a stand is out? Tap Follow on its page and you'll get a notification when it sets out or restocks.

How do I pay? Tap Pay for what I took, pick your items, and pay the owner with Venmo, Cash App or PayPal, or leave cash in the box. Payments go straight to the owner; Porch Stand never touches the money.

Something's wrong with a stand. Tap the … button on its page to report or hide it.

For stand owners

How do I start? Open My Stand, tap Start my stand, and make an account. Add where your stand is, your usual days and hours, how you take payment, and what you sell.

How do I tell people I'm out? Tap Set out now. As things sell, change what's left and tap Post update. Followers get at most one notification every 15 minutes.

What does it cost? Porch Stand is free.

Deleting your data

Open My Stand. Owners tap Delete my account; shoppers tap Delete my data.
