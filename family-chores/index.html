Family Chores

One chore schedule for the whole family

Family Chores puts everybody's jobs for the day in one place. Parents set up who does what on each day of the week. Kids open the app, see their jobs and check them off, and a reminder goes out each day.

Help and questions: email hello@hivebuilt.dev. Please say which phone you use and what you were doing when something went wrong.

Common questions

Do my kids need an email address? No. On the Family tab, tap Get code next to a kid, and the kid types the code into the app on their phone. Codes work once, for 24 hours.

Some of my kids share a tablet. Tap each of those kids on the Family tab and turn on Uses the shared family device. Then tap Get a code for the device and type it in on the tablet. Each kid taps their name to see their jobs.

Can both parents manage the schedule? Yes. Add the other parent on the Family tab and tap Get code next to them. They make their own account, tap I have a parent code and type it in.

How do I change a job for just one day? On the Week tab, tap the job and choose Just this day. Choose Every week to change it from now on.

When does the reminder go out? At the time set on the Family tab, to anyone who still has jobs left.

I forgot my password. Email us from the address you signed up with and we will help.

How do I delete my account? Family tab, Delete account. See the privacy policy for what that removes.
