Privacy Policy

Family Chores · updated November 14, 2026

Family Chores is published by Pierson Willhite (Hivebuilt). This page explains what the app does with your information. In short: we keep only what the chore schedule needs, only your family can see it, and there are no ads and no tracking.

What we keep, and why

  • Parent accounts: an email address and password, used only to sign in.
  • Your family: the family name, the first names or nicknames a parent types in for each person, the jobs on the schedule, which jobs were checked off and when, your time zone and the reminder time.
  • Phones in the family: a random ID for each phone or tablet that joins with a code, and, if you allow notifications, a notification address for that device so the daily reminder can reach it.

This is stored on our server, hosted by Supabase in the United States. Only the parents and devices in your family can see your family's schedule. Reminders are delivered through Expo and Apple's or Google's notification service; they contain the jobs still left that day and, on a shared device, the kids' first names.

Children

Kids use Family Chores under a parent's account. A parent adds each kid by first name or nickname and decides which phones they use. Kids do not make accounts, give an email address, or type in anything other than a one-time code from a parent, and they cannot message anyone. A parent can remove a kid, with their jobs and check-offs, at any time on the Family tab.

Deleting your data

In the app, open the Family tab, then Delete account. If you are the only parent, this permanently deletes the whole family: every person, job, check-off and joined device. If another parent is in the family, only your own account is removed and the family stays with them. Your login is also deleted, unless the same email still signs in to another Hivebuilt app. You can also email us to ask for deletion.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, analytics or tracking, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing.
  • Your email is used only to sign in.

Contact

Questions about privacy: hello@hivebuilt.dev. If this policy changes, the date at the top will change.
