Doubles for Nothing

The dice dare party game

Pick a challenge. Pass the dice around the group. Roll doubles and you're safe. The last person without doubles does the challenge, and there's no backing out.

Help and questions: email hello@hivebuilt.dev. Please say which phone you use and what happened.

How to play

  1. One person taps Start a Game and picks a challenge card, or writes their own.
  2. Everyone else enters the 4-letter room code to join. No accounts needed.
  3. The dice pass from phone to phone. On your turn, tap Roll.
  4. Roll doubles and you're safe. The first person to roll doubles is the winner.
  5. When only two players are left and one rolls doubles, the other gets one roll to match it. Match it and you both keep rolling. Miss it and you lose.
  6. The loser does the challenge.

Common questions

Can I play alone? Yes. In the lobby, tap Add a computer player. Computer players take their own turns.

What if someone stops rolling? After 30 seconds the game rolls for them, so nobody can stall.

What if someone leaves? They're out. If they leave when it's down to the last two, they lose. No backing out.

Are the challenges safe? They're meant to be funny, not harmful. Skip any food someone is allergic to and choose another card. The Family Friendly deck is made for kids.
