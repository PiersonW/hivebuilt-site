Tidy Year

House cleaning plan, all year

Tidy Year builds a cleaning plan for your whole year: a short list every day, each room on its own day of the week, and the deep cleaning spread out so it never piles up. Share it and the kids see their own jobs on their own phones.

Help and questions: email hello@hivebuilt.dev. Please say which phone you use and what you were doing when something went wrong.

Common questions

How does it build my plan? From your bedrooms, bathrooms, square footage, floors, extra rooms, pets and kids, and the days you clean. Weekly jobs for each room share one cleaning day; monthly, 3-month, 6-month and yearly jobs are spread across your cleaning days.

Can I change things? Yes. Rename rooms, add your own jobs, change how often anything repeats, pause or remove jobs, and move or skip a job once. More, Re-spread my year evens everything out again.

How do I give jobs to the family? Add people under More, Family. Then open a job and tap Who to give it to someone every time, just once, or have people take turns. On the Rooms tab, Give room to hands over a whole room.

How do the kids see their jobs? Open More, Share with family. On their phone or tablet, they install Tidy Year, tap Joining your family? Enter a code, type your family code and pick their name. Open Tidy Year on your phone at least once a week so their phones keep three weeks of jobs ahead.

Is my plan backed up? No. Your plan stays on your phone, so deleting the app deletes it. Only the next three weeks of jobs are copied to our server, and only while you share with family.
