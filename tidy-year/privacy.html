Privacy Policy

Tidy Year · updated November 16, 2026

Tidy Year is published by Pierson Willhite (Hivebuilt). In short: your plan stays on your phone unless you choose to share it with your family, and there are no ads, no accounts and no tracking.

What stays on your phone

Your home details, rooms, jobs, family names, check-offs and reminder settings are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

If you share with your family

When you turn on Share with family, your phone sends a copy of the next three weeks of jobs to our server, hosted by Supabase in the United States: each job's name, room, how-to notes and day, who it's for, the first names or nicknames you typed for each person, and which jobs were ticked off. Each phone that shares or joins gets a random ID; no email, password or real name is needed.

Only phones that joined with your family code can see your family's jobs.

Children

Kids can use Tidy Year on their own device under a parent's shared plan. They only type in a family code and pick their name from the list the parent made. They don't make an account, give an email address, or message anyone.

Deleting shared data

On the parent's phone, More, Share with family, Stop sharing permanently deletes the shared copy from our server. On a joined phone, Leave the family plan removes it from that phone. You can also email us to ask for deletion.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your phone. No information is sent to a server to deliver them.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking.
  • Nothing is sold or shared for marketing.

Contact

Questions about privacy: hello@hivebuilt.dev. If this policy changes, the date at the top will change.
