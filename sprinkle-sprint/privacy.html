Privacy Policy

Sprinkle Sprint · updated November 14, 2026

Sprinkle Sprint is published by Pierson Willhite (Hivebuilt). In short: there are no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Practice races stay on your device.

Practice races

Practice races against the computer run entirely on your device. Nothing about them is sent anywhere. The app remembers your name, racer and last race code on your device so you don't have to type them again.

Family races, and what they send

To let several phones race each other, the app uses a game server hosted by Supabase in the United States. When you start or join a family race, it stores:

  • A random ID for your device. The app gets one the first time you start or join a race. It is not tied to your name, email, phone number or Apple ID.
  • The name you type and the racer you pick, so the other racers can see who is who.
  • The race itself: the race code, the race length, how far each racer has gone and who finished first.

Only people in the same race, who joined with its code, can see that race's names and results. A race room is deleted when its last racer leaves.

What we do not do

  • No email, phone number, contacts, location, photos, camera or microphone.
  • No chat or messaging between players.
  • No advertising, analytics, tracking or in-app purchases, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing.

Deleting your data

Leaving a race removes you from it, and the room is deleted when everyone has left. Deleting the app removes the random ID and your saved name from your device. To ask us to delete anything sooner, email us with your race code.

Children

Sprinkle Sprint is made for kids and families. It does not ask for anyone's age, email or real identity, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children. We suggest racers type a first name or nickname. A parent can email us to have a race's information deleted.
