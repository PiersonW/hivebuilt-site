Privacy Policy

Recipe Box · updated November 23, 2026

Recipe Box is published by Pierson Willhite (Hivebuilt). This page explains what the app does with your information. In short: your recipes stay on your phone, there are no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

What stays on your phone

Your recipes, photos, notes, favorites and settings are stored only on your device. We cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

What is sent, and why

  • Recipe websites. When you save a link, or share a page to Recipe Box, the app loads that page from the website directly, the same way a web browser would. For a Pinterest pin, the app asks Pinterest's public pin service which recipe page the pin links to. For an Instagram or Facebook post, it loads the post's public preview.
  • Screenshots and photos, only the ones you choose, are sent to our server and read by Anthropic's Claude AI model, which returns the recipe as text. When a web page has no recipe card, the page's text is sent the same way. We do not keep the pictures or text after they are read.
  • A random number created by the app is sent with each picture or page so we can limit how many recipes one phone reads per day. We keep only a daily count for it. It is not linked to you.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, sign-in, names or email addresses are collected by the app.
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Contact

Questions about privacy: hello@hivebuilt.dev. If this policy changes, the date at the top will change.
