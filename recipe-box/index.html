Recipe Box

Save and bake your recipes

Recipe Box keeps every recipe you love on your phone, sorted and searchable, so they open even with no signal. Save recipes from websites, screenshots, cookbook pages and handwritten recipe cards, or type them in yourself.

Help and questions: email hello@hivebuilt.dev. If a recipe didn't save right, please include the link to it.

Common questions

Recipe Box isn't in my Share menu. Tap the Share button on a recipe page, scroll the row of apps all the way to the end, tap More, and turn on Recipe Box. You can drag it to the front of the list while you're there.

A website didn't save. Some sites block apps from reading their pages. Take one or more screenshots of the recipe and add them from Add › Choose from Photos. For a long recipe, pick up to 6 screenshots at once, in order.

It read a handwritten card wrong. Tap Edit to fix anything. The original picture is kept at the bottom of the recipe, so you can always check it. Amounts it couldn't read show as [?].

How do cups and grams work? On any recipe, choose Cups or Grams under the ingredients. Weights come from a standard baking ingredient weight chart. Eggs, spices and anything it doesn't know stay as written.

Do I need internet? Only to read a new recipe from a website, screenshot or photo. Everything you've saved opens offline.

Are my recipes backed up? Not yet. Recipes are kept on your phone, so deleting the app deletes them. You can share any recipe as text from More › Share.
