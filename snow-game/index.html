🎮 How to play

📱 On a phone: turn it sideways. Left thumb anywhere on the left steers (push up to push along, pull down to tuck; in the air it spins and flips). Right thumb: Jump (hold to jump higher), Grab (in the air; on the snow it shoves whoever's beside you), Grind (hold near a rail), Brake. ☰ is the menu.
