Privacy Policy

Road Rivals · updated November 14, 2026

Road Rivals is published by Pierson Willhite (Hivebuilt). In short: there are no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

What the app sends, and why

To keep everyone in the car on one leaderboard, the app uses a game server hosted by Supabase in the United States. When you start or join a trip, it stores:

  • A random ID for your phone. The app gets one the first time you start or join a trip. It is not tied to your name, email, phone number or Apple ID.
  • The name you type, so the others in your trip can see who's winning.
  • Your finds: what you marked in each game (for example "Utah plate" or "red car"), the points, and when you found it.

Only people in the same trip, who joined with its code, can see that trip's names and finds. Solo games stay on your phone and are never sent.

What we do not do

  • No email, phone number, contacts, location, photos, camera or microphone.
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing.

Deleting your data

Tapping a find again removes it. Deleting the app removes the random ID from your phone, and the server can no longer link anything to you. To have a trip and its finds deleted from the server, email us the trip code.

Children

Road Rivals is made for families. It does not ask for anyone's age and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Players type only a first name or nickname.

Contact

Questions about privacy: hello@hivebuilt.dev. If this policy changes, the date at the top will change.
