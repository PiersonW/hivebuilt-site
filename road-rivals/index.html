Road Rivals

Everyone in the car, one leaderboard

Road Rivals turns a long drive into a game. Everyone in the car plays on their own phone, spotting license plates, letters, numbers, animals and more, and one live leaderboard shows who's winning.

Help and questions: email hello@hivebuilt.dev. Please say which phone you use and what happened.

How to play

  1. One person types their name and taps Start a Trip. The app shows a 5-letter trip code.
  2. Everyone else taps Join with a Code and types it in. No accounts needed.
  3. Pick any game and tap things as you see them. Every player has their own board, so someone else's find never takes yours away.
  4. Check the Leaderboard to see who's in the lead, game by game.
  5. When you arrive, the host taps End Trip for Everyone and every phone shows the final results.

No one else along? Tap Play Solo.

The games

  • License Plates: all 50 states, Canadian provinces and bonus plates. Each new plate is worth more than the last, up to 10.
  • Alphabet Game: find A to Z on signs, in order. J, Q, X and Z count double.
  • Numbers: 1 to 100, plus triples and quadruples for big bonuses.
  • Color Count: count cars of each color.
  • Nature Spotter, Vehicle Spotter, Road Signs and Seek and Find: rare sightings like a bear or a covered bridge score big.

Common questions

Does it work without signal? Yes. Finds are saved on your phone and sync when you're back in range.

Made a mistake? Tap a found item again to un-mark it.

Should the driver play? No. Passengers only. Drivers, eyes on the road.
