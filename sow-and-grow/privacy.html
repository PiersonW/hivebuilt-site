Privacy Policy

Sow & Grow: Garden Planner · updated November 14, 2026

Sow & Grow is published by Pierson Willhite (Hivebuilt). This page explains what the app does with your information. In short: your gardens stay on your phone, there are no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

What stays on your phone

Your gardens, plantings, schedules, to-dos, garden walk notes, photos you take in the app, and your settings are stored only on your device. We cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

What is sent, and why

  • Your garden's location, rounded to about a kilometre, is sent to Open-Meteo to get weather, historical temperatures (for frost dates) and elevation. Town names you type are sent to Open-Meteo's place search. If you use Use where I am now, the phone's location is used once to find your garden and is not tracked afterwards.
  • Your garden's postal code (United States only) is sent to phzmapi.org to look up your official USDA hardiness zone.
  • Seed packet photos, only when you choose to photograph or pick one, are sent to our server and read by Anthropic's Claude AI model, which returns the growing details printed on the packet. The photo is not stored by us after it is read. The details read from the packet (crop, variety, days to maturity, spacing and similar) are kept in a shared seed library, without the photo and without anything that identifies you or your device, so other gardeners can find that variety by name without photographing it again. A random number created by the app is sent with each photo so we can limit how many packets one phone reads per day; it is not linked to you.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, sign-in, names or email addresses are collected by the app.
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing.
  • Reminders are scheduled on your phone; no notification service receives your to-do list.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Contact

Questions about privacy: hello@hivebuilt.dev. If this policy changes, the date at the top will change.
